# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 2026

Welcome to my digital garden! I value privacy and believe in keeping the web simple, clean, and non-intrusive. This policy explains how data is handled when you visit this website.

---

### TL;DR (The Human Summary)
* **No Cookies:** This website does not use tracking, advertising, or targeting cookies.
* **No Personal Data Selling:** I do not collect, monetize, or sell your personal information.
* **Minimal & Anonymous Stats:** Any analytics used are strictly aggregated and anonymous to help me see which notes are helpful.

### 1. Cookies & Local Storage
This website does not store tracking cookies on your device. 

The only data stored locally in your browser is your preferred visual theme (Light Mode or Dark Mode) via standard browser `localStorage`. This stays entirely on your device and is never sent to a server.

*(If using privacy-first analytics like Plausible, Umami, or Pirsch)*

This tool operates **without cookies** and **does not track individual visitors** across the web or log personally identifiable information (PII) like full IP addresses.
